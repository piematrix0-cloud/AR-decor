FAQs

Common Questions

What areas do you serve? +

We are based in Daltonganj and serve all major Jharkhand cities including Ranchi, Deoghar, Hazaribagh, and surrounding regions.

How long does an interior project take? +

Typically 4–8 weeks depending on the scope. We provide a detailed timeline during the initial consultation.

Do you offer free consultations? +

Absolutely! The first consultation is complimentary — we'll discuss your vision and provide initial recommendations.

Can I see a 3D model before work begins? +

Yes, we create detailed 3D renderings so you can visualize the final outcome before any construction starts.
